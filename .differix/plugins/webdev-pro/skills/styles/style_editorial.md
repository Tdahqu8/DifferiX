# Style: Editorial / Magazine

- Background: Warm cream or paper-like texture (e.g., #faf8f5).
- Colors: Deep charcoal text (#1a1a1a), one muted accent (e.g., dark forest green or oxblood red).
- Typography: Elegant Serif fonts for headings (e.g., Playfair Display, Merriweather). Clean Sans-Serif for body text.
- Layout: Asymmetrical. Use large, oversized quote marks or drop caps.
- Content: Wide margins, lots of negative space.
- Images: If any, style them with sepia or grayscale filters and thin, elegant borders.
- NO heavy shadows. Use thin hairline dividers (`border-bottom: 1px solid #1a1a1a`) to separate sections.
